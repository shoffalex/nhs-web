File share

Chapter documents and forms. On the original site this section is a members-only file library, so the files themselves are not published publicly.

Members only. The chapter's file library is restricted to logged-in members. If you are a member and cannot find a document, check Schoology or email Ms. Melton.
Documents

What lives here

These are the document types the chapter shares with members through the year. Each entry links to a file once it has been added to the documents/ folder.

Membership application Available starting February 2nd each year, for the upcoming school year. Not uploaded
Teacher recommendation form Handed out in February. Completed by teachers — students do not request these. Not uploaded
Service hour log For recording community service completed outside of school. Not uploaded
Meeting agendas and minutes Posted after each monthly meeting by the secretary. Not uploaded
Chapter bylaws and member expectations Standards members agree to uphold across all four pillars. Not uploaded
Induction ceremony information Details for the late April / early May induction. Not uploaded
Adding a file. Drop the file into the documents/ folder next to this page, then change that row's <div class="file-row"> to an <a href="documents/your-file.pdf"> and swap the grey Not uploaded pill for a blue Download one.

Can't find what you need?
