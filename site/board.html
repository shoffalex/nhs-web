Meet the advisory board

Welcome to the NHS Advisory Board! We are committed to supporting you and answering any questions you may have at any time. Our mission is to promote the core pillars of the National Honor Society: scholarship, service, leadership, and character. Feel free to reach out — we are here to assist you on your journey.

How to reach the board

The board is available to answer member questions at any time. The most reliable routes are the chapter sponsor's email, monthly meetings, and chapter posts on Schoology and Instagram.
